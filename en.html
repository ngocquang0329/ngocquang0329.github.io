Hello,

I am Kien Minh Nguyen Ngoc Quang, a Person-Centred therapist. I would be genuinely privileged to accompany you as you explore and work through the difficulties you are currently experiencing.

When working with clients facing psychological challenges, I strive to offer a genuine, accepting, and empathetic presence. Throughout this process, I dedicate myself to being attentive, receptive, listening closely, and seeking to understand your lived experiences and reflections.

In doing so, I hope to provide you with a safe and confidential space where you are free to experience and utilize our time in whatever way feels most meaningful to you.

Crisis Support

This online counselling service is not suitable for life-threatening emergencies or acute crisis situations requiring immediate intervention.

If you are in acute distress, feel unable to keep yourself safe, or are having active thoughts of suicide, please reach out immediately to a trusted person, call the medical emergency services at 115, or contact your local hospital emergency department.

Ethical Practice

My clinical practice complies with the Ethical Framework for the Counselling Professions of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP), adapted appropriately to the Vietnamese context.

Clinical Supervision

In accordance with BACP standards, professional counsellors maintain regular supervision (a minimum of 1.5 hours per month) to ensure continuous professional development and to protect client safety.

I practice under the regular clinical supervision of an experienced therapist based in the United Kingdom. These sessions focus on reviewing and refining my clinical practice to ensure it remains as helpful and supportive to your therapeutic journey as possible.

During supervision, aspects of our therapeutic work may be discussed. However, all material is entirely anonymized, omitting non-essential personal details and safeguarding your identity at all times.

Process

Step 1: Free Consultation

You are welcome to read through the information provided on this website or from any other channels you have access to.

If you wish, we can arrange an initial online consultation, free of charge, lasting 10 to 15 minutes. To schedule, please send an email to kienminhcounselling@gmail.com. In your message, feel free to briefly share your reasons and hopes for seeking therapy so I can learn a little more about you; we will then agree on a suitable time to connect.

During this conversation, we can discuss your expectations, explore what I can and cannot offer, address any questions you may have, or discuss anything you would like to know regarding how I work.

Step 2: Agreement & Consent

If following our initial consultation you feel that I am not the right match for you, the process concludes there. You do not need to provide an explanation. This decision will not affect any future relationship we might have, including any potential therapeutic engagement should you decide to return.

If you would like to work together, or wish to explore a few sessions before making a longer-term decision, we will sign a working agreement. Even after signing, you retain the complete freedom to end therapy at any time without any obligation to justify or compensate. The primary purpose of this agreement is to establish clear mutual commitments regarding your rights and confidentiality.

Step 3: First Session

To schedule our first full session, please email me at kienminhcounselling@gmail.com. Once our session time is confirmed, please complete the session fee transfer within 24 hours to secure the booking. I will then send you the Google Meet link via email.

During this first session, and throughout all future sessions, you are encouraged to use our time and space in whichever way you find most helpful and comfortable for yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Person-Centred therapy be effective for me?

A substantial body of scientific research demonstrates that Person-Centred (Client-Centred) therapy is effective across a wide range of concerns, including depression and anxiety, for many diverse clients.

However, empirical findings on therapeutic efficacy reflect general, aggregate averages. In my view, no scientific data can guarantee with certainty that a specific therapeutic approach will work for any particular individual.

Throughout my professional journey, I have witnessed clients experience profound, positive transformation through the presence and support of Person-Centred therapists. At the same time, I have also encountered instances where this approach did not feel effective or produced little noticeable change.

In reality, therapeutic change is shaped by a complex interplay of factors arising from the client, the therapist, and the therapeutic orientation itself. While we cannot always pinpoint what facilitates or hinders movement, it is clear that no single modality or therapist is universally suited to every person or every issue.

Ultimately, while it takes time and effort, the most reliable and meaningful way to know whether this approach works for you is to experience it firsthand.
